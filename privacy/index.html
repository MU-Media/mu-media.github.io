Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

This policy explains what MU Media Insights (the "Service") collects when a TikTok account is connected, and how it is used.

1. Data we collect

  • Profile info: open ID, display name, username, avatar URL.
  • Profile stats: follower, following, like and video counts.
  • Video list: IDs, titles/descriptions, creation time, duration, cover URL, share URL and view, like, comment and share counts of the account's own videos.
  • OAuth tokens issued by TikTok, needed to read the data above.

We do not collect passwords, messages, contact lists or data about other users.

2. How we use it

Only to show the team how its own accounts and videos perform over time (dashboards and a daily summary). We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties and do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored on a server we operate in the European Union. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and never written to logs. Access is limited to the team.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account is connected. When an account is disconnected or deletion is requested, its tokens are deleted immediately and its stored statistics within 30 days.

5. Your choices

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or ask us to delete your data at tinylabsdev@gmail.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
